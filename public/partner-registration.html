GoodResults To You.

The Kingdom of GOD advances through men and women whose hearts are committed to seeing the Gospel reach every nation. GOD has always used willing partners to establish His purposes on the earth.

Envoys of Christ Love Ministry International, The Jesus Generation Church and GoodResults Worldwide, warmly invites you to become a Kingdom Partner in this GOD-given assignment of reconciling humanity back to GOD through the teaching of Christ Jesus. By partnering with this vision, you become a co-laborer with Christ in reaching souls, restoring lives, equipping believers, supporting missions, and expanding the Gospel to the nations.

“He which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully.” — 2 Corinthians 9:6
Read the full invitation letter

The work of GOD is not sustained by one person alone, but by faithful men and women who answer the call to stand together for the advancement of His Kingdom. Throughout Scripture, GOD has always raised partners who willingly supported His work with their prayers, resources, and service.

Your partnership is not merely a financial contribution—it is a covenant investment into the advancement of GOD's Kingdom. Every seed you sow helps us preach the Gospel, disciple believers, support evangelistic outreaches, equip leaders, care for communities, and bring hope to lives through the transforming power of Jesus Christ.

And again,

“Give, and it shall be given unto you; good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over, shall men give into your bosom.” — Luke 6:38

We prayerfully invite you to partner with us according to the grace GOD has placed upon your heart.

As you partner with this ministry (ENVOYS OF CHRIST LOVE), we believe God will:

  • Open supernatural doors of favor.
  • Release divine provision and increase.
  • Establish the work of your hands.
  • Grant protection over you and your household.
  • Cause you to become a blessing to many generations.
  • Remember your seed and reward your faithfulness according to His Word.

Every partner is valued, appreciated, and remembered in our prayers. Together we are building GOD's Kingdom and raising a generation that knows Christ and makes Him known.

No gift is too small when it is given with love, faith, and a willing heart. Every seed sown into GOD's Kingdom becomes a testimony of His faithfulness.

We also invite those who may not be able to commit monthly but desire to support the vision through one-time offerings, projects, missions, or special Kingdom assignments. Your partnership is valuable and appreciated.

May the LORD remember your sacrifice, multiply your harvest, and cause His grace to abound toward you in every area of your life.

If the LORD has stirred your heart to become one of our covenant partners, we warmly welcome you into this Kingdom family. May the LORD establish the work of your hands, enlarge your territory, supply all your needs according to His riches in glory, and cause you to abound in every good work.

Thank you for standing with us as we take the Gospel of Jesus Christ to the nations.
